punchCraft glove check

Connects a FightCamp v1 glove to this PC through the browser and runs the same start-up the tablet app runs. Blue is left, red is right. Wake one glove (tap it), click its button, pick FightCam, throw a few punches, and leave it connected for a minute — a glove this PC is not paired with drops between 30 and 45 seconds. Then do the other glove.
